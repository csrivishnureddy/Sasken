Awards & Recognitions

Celebrating excellence, innovation, and commitment at Sasken.

ICAI Sustainability Reporting Award for Excellence in BRSR Reporting

Sasken has been recognized by the Institute of Chartered Accountants of India (ICAI) for Excellence in BRSR Reporting in the 'Service Sector - Small Cap' category at the 4th ICAI Sustainability Reporting Awards 2024–25. This recognition highlights our unwavering commitment to sustainability reporting and responsible business practices, solidifying Sasken’s position as a leader in advancing these critical initiatives.

ICSI Certificate of Appreciation for Excellence in CSR

Sasken was recognized at the ICSI National Awards for Excellence in Corporate Governance. The Institute of Company Secretaries Of India honored Sasken with the Certificate Of Appreciation at the 10th ICSI CSR Excellence Award, held at New Delhi on 19 December, 2025. The ICSI CSR Excellence Awards, instituted in 2016, aims to recognize and promote the execution of CSR amongst Indian corporates. Based on an intensive evaluation process, Sasken was recognized for persistent and innovative efforts in promoting CSR. 

EcoVadis Bronze Medal

Sasken successfully completed EcoVadis 2025 Sustainability Assessment and has retained the Bronze Medal for the 2nd year, placing our organization in the 79th percentile globally. The assessment covers four comprehensive sustainability themes: Environment, Labor & Human Rights, Ethics, and Sustainable Procurement. This recognition speaks strongly about our transparency, governance, and operational integrity, and places us among top rated companies in Sustainability. It reinforces our commitment to sustainable and responsible business practices and strengthens our credibility with customers, partners, and future stakeholders.

Sustainable Organization Award 2025

At the 7th Edition Sustainability Summit & Awards 2025, hosted by UBS Forum on November 27, 2025, Sasken was honored with the “Sustainable Organization Award 2025” for its outstanding commitment to sustainable practices.

1st Runner-Up Award in the Sustainability Leader of the Year Category

At the CREFM Masterstroke Awards 2025, held during the Clean India Show 2025 at the Mumbai Exhibition Center on November 26, 2025, Sasken received the 1st Runner-Up award in the “Sustainability Leader of the Year” category for its excellence in sustainable leadership and innovation.

LinkedIn Top Companies in India 2025 under the ‘Midsize Employers’ Category

Sasken was featured in the LinkedIn Top Companies in India 2025 list for the first time under the ‘midsize employers’ category. This annual list by LinkedIn News highlights the best workplaces for professionals to grow their careers. The ranking, published by LinkedIn News, is determined by a carefully weighted methodology using exclusive LinkedIn data analyzing the career paths of millions of professionals globally.

Great Places to Work: Recognition awarded by Great Place To Work®

Sasken won the prestigious 'Great Place To Work®' Certification for the second time. This recognition is a testament to our commitment to diversity, equity, and inclusive practices. We strive to create a harmonious environment for our employees, encourage them to prioritize their physical and mental well-being, and aim to strike a balance between their professional and personal lives through a flexible working model.

2025 Avtar and Seramount Best Companies for Women in India (IT)

Sasken Technologies has been named as “One of the 2025 Best Companies for Women in India” and is listed among India's Top 100 Companies for Women in 2025. This recognition from Avtar and Seramount fuels our passion for building a workplace where inclusion is at our core. Our HR policies have enabled women at Sasken to achieve work-life balance and pursue fulfilling careers, emphasizing inclusive practices and equitable opportunities.

Karnataka Best Employer Brand Awards 2025

Sasken has been awarded the title of the ‘Karnataka Best Employer Brand Awards 2025’. The award features the top organizations from Karnataka’s different cities and regions who are exemplary in HR and have used marketing communications effectively for human resources development. The ‘Karnataka Best Employer Brand Awards 2025’ was endorsed by the CHRO Asia, and the certification was provided by the World Federation of HR Professionals.

PMI Runner-up Award in the Community Category

Sasken has been recognized at the prestigious PMI South Asia Chapter 2024 Awards by Project Management Institute, securing the Runner-up position in the "Community" category. This recognition highlights the excellence in project management practices in CSR initiatives and best-in-class execution. A special spotlight shines on our ongoing "Nayana: A Vision of Hope!" initiative, spearheaded in collaboration with Vittala Institute of Ophthalmology.
This initiative focuses on providing critical ophthalmic care to rural and underprivileged communities across Karnataka through a mobile ophthalmology clinic. Operating for 23 days each month, the clinic addresses Diabetic Retinopathy in remote areas, where access to medical services is limited.
