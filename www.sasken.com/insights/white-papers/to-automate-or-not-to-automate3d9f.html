To Automate or Not to Automate

Written by Debabrata Sahoo | Oct 26, 2022 6:45:20 AM

With the advancement of technology, significant investment in research and development, and increased production capacity, we are constantly surrounded by more sophisticated and novel objects. Consumers' expectations are evolving. We desire instant access to better and more recent items. Additionally, this has compelled the market, whether it be for hardware or software, to be extremely competitive in terms of cost, value, and time to market. Only with a well-defined process and infrastructure in place that can deliver more quickly, accurately, and affordably can this be accomplished. So, the solution is automation.
