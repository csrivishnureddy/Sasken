Sasken Buzz: Won 5G-based Telematics Platform Project | Sasken

Written by Admin | Jul 3, 2021 7:23:00 AM

Sasken enjoys the reputation of being a leader in delivering solutions in the product engineering and digital segments across multiple verticals. We are partnering with a Tier 1 supplier of automotive telematics platforms in developing their Network Access Device (NAD). We are building a solution on the next-gen 5G chipset and developing this platform from the ground up, incorporating customizable and cost-effective design that can adapt to changes in technology. Sasken’s expertise in telematics, modem and Linux drivers, as well as middleware, played a crucial role in being identified as the partner of choice. Sasken is enabling the customer to launch the first-of-its-kind telematics platform with 5G support.
