Developing IoT Platform with Real-time Tracking | Sasken

Written by Admin | Dec 10, 2018 1:14:00 PM

Sasken will develop an IoT platform for real-time tracking and visualization of paving operations for the world's largest construction equipment manufacturer. The IoT platform will support live streaming and processing of data from two million assets and 50 million events per day. The solution will enable the customer to monetize its products through aftermarket services and increase stickiness with its customers. Our engineers will develop the web and mobile applications for the customer’s dealers to help manage high-value assets with the aid of responsive micro-service widgets that enable data visualization.
