Golden Peacock Award (2019) - CSR Initiatives | Sasken

Written by Admin | Mar 1, 2020 5:00:00 AM

The 14th International Conference on Corporate Social Responsibility was held recently to commemorate various organizations and their contributions towards society via their CSR initiatives. Sasken received the prestigious Golden Peacock award, as a special commendation for the multiple CSR activities undertaken in the year 2019. A committee of qualified personnel was involved in the final selection, which was chaired by Honorable Justice M.N Venkatachaliah, former Chief Justice of India. This event not only assessed the financial contributions towards CSR initiatives, but also their sustainability and long term viability. This award serves as apt recognition for Sasken’s CSR initiatives and will encourage and motivate the organization towards enhancing and pursuing selfless drives to enable a better society.
