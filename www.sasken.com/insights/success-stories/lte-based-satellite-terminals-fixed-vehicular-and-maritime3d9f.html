Business Success Stories - LTE-based Satellite Terminal | Sasken

Written by Admin | Feb 17, 2018 5:00:00 AM

Scope

To design and develop the satellite terminals for Fixed, Vehicular and Maritime domain supporting LTE over satellite

Solution

  • Requirement analysis, Feasibility of requirements, Concepts discussion and analysis, System Architecture Design, Antenna design (Internal, External and Tracking antenna)
  • HW and SW Design and Implementation for both different terminals
  • Electro mechanics engineering, DFM & product validation verification
  • Schematics and PCB layout design
  • LTE for Satcom modem integration with RF and Antenna, Wi-Fi, GPS, USB-C, POTS/Fax/Voice over IP, SIP/IMS, Transcoding of codecs, Ipxx, Certifications
  • SW tools – Firmware upgrade, Maintenance tools, Development and maintenance debugging tools, Production testing, Logging
  • Acceptance, Factory acceptance, over the air satellite testing
  • 0-serie prototype manufacturing and verification
  • Manufacturing ramp-up support and documentation

Impact

  • With a multi-site development team, Sasken delivered excellent power management and call quality for one of the first implementations of LTE over satellite
