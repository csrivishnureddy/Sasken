      Mechanics Design

      Sasken Finland provides services in the area of mechanics design, design for manufacturing and testability, production tester system development. Sasken Finland's experienced mechanics design team have long experience in consumer products and rugged device development. Our teams are equipped with the latest design software tools and a wide range of high-end measurement devices for environmental, mechanical & material measurements and testing.

      Sasken Finland offers mechanics design expertise in the following areas:

      • Design for plastics, injection molding and milling
      • Design for metals, sheet metal and die-casting
      • Design for manufacturing and automated assembly
      • Mechanical design of components, systems and products
      • Rapid prototyping, rapid tooling, and rapid manufacturing support
      • In-house and external partners

      Expertise:

      • Surface modelling
      • 3D Virtual Prototyping/Products
      • Mechanical Engineering
      • Simulations
      • Mold filling analysis
      • Tool design for plastic injection molding
      • Thermal design
      • Test equipment design
      • Tools expertise:
        • CatiaV5
        • Experience also in I-deas
        • Pro-E
        • Solid Works

        • Flotherm
        • Ansys
        • C-mold
