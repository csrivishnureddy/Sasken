      Leadership Team

      Mikko Keskilammi

      AVP – Practice, Sasken Finland

      Download Profile

      Mikko Keskilammi is responsible for hardware design team and test labs located at Sasken Kaustinen site.

      Mikko helps Sasken’s Antenna Engineers and Customers find solutions to improve antenna performance.

      Before joining Sasken in 2006, Mikko worked as a Senior Researcher in Tampere University of Technology where he researched on UHF RFID antennas.

      Mikko actively follows the development of new technologies and builds co-operation with different research organizations in the wireless field.

      Mikko holds an M.Sc. (Tech.) and a D.Sc. (Tech.) degrees in Electronics Engineering from Tampere University of Technology.

      Mikko loves to enjoy good food with family and friends. He is a passionate mountain biker and likes to watch soccer and hockey with his son.
