Sasken quarterly results announced: Q4 FY19 | Sasken

Written by Admin | Apr 24, 2019 4:00:00 AM

Bengaluru, April 23, 2019;Sasken Technologies Limited (formerly Sasken Communication Technologies Limited) (BSE: 532663, NSE: SASKEN) today announced its consolidated results as per Ind AS for the quarter and year ended March 31, 2019.

Rajiv C Mody, Chairman, Managing Director & CEO, Sasken Technologies Limited remarked: We have been able to resolve the overruns in our fixed price program which we had flagged in the previous quarter. Our customer has compensated us for the additional work executed and continues to value us as a strategic partner. The positive upshot is after factoring this one time impact we have posted an organic revenue growth of 4% this quarter. As part of our business transformation journey, we remain focused on growing our strategic accounts. We have new business wins emanating from marquee customers and will endeavour to scale them.

Performance Snapshot for the Quarter: Q4 FY 19

  • Consolidated Revenues for Q4 FY 19 at ₹ 135.54 Crore
    • Up 11.6 % sequentially over the previous quarter
    • Up 5.3 % YoY from Q4 FY 18
  • Consolidated EBIDTA for Q4 FY 19 at ₹ 24.44 Crore
    • Up 93.2 % sequentially over the previous quarter
    • Up 13.6 % YoY from Q4 FY 18
  • Consolidated PAT for Q4 FY 19 at ₹ 27.45 Crore
    • Up 51.9 % sequentially over the previous quarter
    • Up 5.7 % YoY from Q4 FY 18
    • PAT Margins for the quarter at 20.3 %

Performance Snapshot for the Financial Year 2019

  • Consolidated Revenues for FY 19 at ₹ 504.31 Crore
    • Up 0.3 % from FY 18
  • Consolidated EBIDTA for FY 19 at ₹ 71.13 Crore
    • Up 2.4 % from FY 18
  • Consolidated PAT for FY 19 at ₹ 90.42 Crore
    • Up 9.7 % from FY 18
    • PAT Margins for the year at 17.9 %

Key Business metrics for the Quarter: Q4 FY 19

  • Software services revenues for Q4 FY 19 at ₹ 135.54 Crore
    • Up 11.6 % sequentially over the previous quarter
    • Up 5.3 % YoY from Q4 FY 18
  • Consolidated EBIDTA margins were at 18.0 %.
  • Revenue contribution from
    • The Top five customers stood at 56.3 % and
    • From Top 10 customers at 70.1 %
  • Consolidated EPS was at ₹ 16.11 for the quarter
  • Added 6 new customers during the quarter taking the total number of active customers to 94

Key Business metrics for the Financial Year 2019

  • Software services revenues for FY 19 at ₹ 504.31 Crore
    • Up 0.3 % from FY 18
  • Consolidated EBIDTA margins were at 14.1 %.
  • Consolidated EPS was at ₹ 52.92 for the year

About Sasken
Sasken is a specialist in Product Engineering and Digital Transformation providing concept-to-market, chip-to-cognition R&D services to global leaders in Semiconductor, Automotive, Industrials, Smart Devices & Wearables, Enterprise Grade Devices, SatCom, and Transportation industries. For over 30 years and with multiple patents, Sasken has transformed the businesses of 100+ Fortune 500 companies, powering more than a billion devices through its services and IP.
