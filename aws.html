WHY CHOOSE SASKEN?

Sasken is a specialist in Product Engineering and Digital Transformation providing concept-to-market, chip-to-cognition R&D services to global leaders in Semiconductor, Automotive, Industrials, Consumer Electronics, Enterprise Devices, Satcom, Telecom, and Transportation industries. For over 30+ years and with 70+ patents, Sasken has transformed the businesses of 100+ Fortune 500 companies, powering more than a billion devices through its services and IP. 
Sasken is driven by a passionate, technology-focused, and futuristic community of talented, certified, and experienced engineers. Customers have an edge over their competition as Sasken’s Engineering Center nurtures budding engineers, trains, and prepares them for the market.
