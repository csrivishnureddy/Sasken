Privacy Policy

    1. 1. Introduction

      Your privacy is important to us. Sasken Technologies Limited and its group companies hereinafter referred to as ‘Sasken’ are committed to respecting your privacy while using our website. This Sasken Data Privacy Policy (“Policy”) defines the requirements to ensure compliance with the applicable data privacy laws and regulations applicable to Sasken’s collection, use, and transmission of Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data for Information collected by us about you.

      Protecting the privacy rights of data subjects and safeguarding their Personal Data is now being treated as a basic right of an individual and a legal requirement in many parts of the world. Sasken, as a global organization, respects the privacy of data subjects and is committed to complying with applicable data privacy laws and legislations (including but not limited to E.U. General Data Protection Regulation 2016/679, California Consumer Privacy Act, Data Protection Act 2018 (U.K.), Information Technology Act 2000 read along with the Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011 and other applicable privacy laws to the extent that they apply to Sasken's data processing and business operations) (the “Data Privacy Laws”).

    2. 2. Policy Statement

      This Policy is designed to explain and set out Sasken's procedures and policies when processing Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data (the meaning of which are set out below) by the organization. This Policy defines the requirements to ensure compliance with the Data Privacy Laws applicable to Sasken's collection, use, and transmission of Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data wherever applicable.

    3. 3. Scope

      The scope of this Policy applies to Sasken, its affiliates, business partners, employees, and Third Parties providing services to Sasken. It involves Processing (including but not limited to collection, storage/retention, usage, transfer, and disposal) of Personal Data and Sensitive Personal Data of Sasken's current and ex-employees, prospective future employees, current, prospective, and ex-customers, current and previous suppliers, website visitors, sub-contractors, and visitors, by Sasken during the course of its business activities.

      Sasken acts as a Data Controller with respect to any Personal Data or Sensitive Personal Data it holds about you. Sasken is responsible for ensuring that it uses your Personal Data in compliance with the Data Privacy Laws. The relevant entities that may act as the Data Controller are listed in Section 22 of this Policy.

      This Policy sets out the basis on which any Personal Data, Personal Information and Sensitive Personal Data about you that you provide to us, that we create, or that we obtain about you from other sources, will be processed by us.
