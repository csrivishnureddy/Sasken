Commercialization of Autonomous Driving (Japan) | Sasken

Written by Admin | Oct 6, 2020 12:14:00 PM

A Japanese automotive company entrusted Sasken with the responsibility of incorporating a chatbot to provide real-time feedback by interpreting the driver’s behavior behind the wheel while commuting. The customer and their OEM partner were impressed by the data-driven approach the solution took towards providing reliable insights. Expertise in Linux and TI platforms, multimedia, and accurate machine learning algorithms ensured the delivery of a solution that was more feature heavy when compared to competing products. Sasken will be closely working on the production of this solution’s future variants and will also incorporate unique features such as drowsiness detection even while wearing a face mask, and a karaoke feature. These will serve as differentiators for the customer’s commercial ADAS solutions for their OEM partner.
