Deploying Smart Connectivity in the UK | Sasken

Written by Admin | Dec 10, 2018 1:14:00 PM

Sasken is enabling a UK-based Automotive electronics customer with a suite of intuitive mobile apps and cloud-based analytics. Sasken will enable the customer to add smart connectivity and deploy an OBD-II dongle to monitor battery vitals, TPMS, dash camera, etc. These can be accessed securely from the cloud. Sasken will also develop a real-time analytics engine using Xamarin to analyze this data and present it on the user’s smartphone. Sasken’s solution will enable customer to continue offering aftermarket automotive products that are digitally smarter, cloud-based, and highly differentiated.
