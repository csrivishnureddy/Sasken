Project Update: Android Upgrade for Asian Electronics | Sasken

Written by Admin | Feb 1, 2020 5:00:00 AM

Sasken has constantly displayed a proficient track record in providing security management for Android versions to several leading OEMs. Leveraging this expertise in the Android segment, an Asian Electronics manufacturer has been able to plan and implement the Android upgrades of their devices for the next two years. Sasken is enabling the customer to upgrade their devices, which are based on various chipsets, from Android 9 to 10, while ensuring that the devices are secure for enterprise usage. Sasken will also be helping the customer to successfully launch their device on a leading Japanese telecom operator, complying with stringent operator customization requirements.
