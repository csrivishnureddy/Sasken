Delivering market insights through mobile apps

Written by Admin | Sep 26, 2016 4:00:00 AM

Scope

Sasken engaged with a retail analytics startup based of Bay area to develop a HTML5 mobile web application to visualize market insights

Solution

  •  Mobile Web application using HTML5, D3.JS, Rickshaw.JS, Cross filters and Knockout.js
  • Application supported on Android and Windows RT tablets
  • Application enables a retailer to visualize
    • Product/price range comparisons.
    • Different cuts of products.
    • Products grouped based on Brands and plotted using bar charts.
    • Merchant-wise cross filters and legends.

Impact

  •  Helped the customer to complete the development of the mobile web application with TTM requirements
  • Equips the retail customer with market insights on a mobile device
