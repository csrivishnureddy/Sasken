Enabled automotive Tier 1 launches IVI solutions

Written by Admin | Sep 10, 2019 4:00:00 AM

Scope: Customer was developing a next-gen IVI solution and was looking for a partner to develop GStreamer based Multimedia framework for local playback application and licensing of codecs for ISDB-T 1 segment Japanese Mobile TV application

Solution: 

  • Development and integration of GStreamer Plugin for Audio decoders and parsers
  • Licensed Audio & Video decoders
    • MPEG 1/2/2.5 Layer 3, AAC-LC, WMA, FLAC decoder on ARM Cortex-A8
    • MPEG2 AAC + SBR and H.264 BP ARIB compliant decoders on DSP (C674x+)
    • WMA decoder and ASF parser on Android StageFright / ARM Cortex-A15
  • Porting and integration of decoders into GStreamer framework for local playback app

Impact:

  • Automated delivery of applications across multiple environments
  • Faster provisioning of environments on cloud in few hours
