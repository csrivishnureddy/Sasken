Better in-vehicle radio functionality, app support

Written by Admin | Sep 26, 2016 4:00:00 AM

Scope

  • System specification, validation and productization of multimedia subsystem including internet based radio apps for an IVI system across model variants (MY16, MY17 etc.)

Solution

  • Developed IVI system based on Renesas Tri Core A9 processor, running Monte Vista Linux OS, supporting Gstreamer Media Player & integration of OEM specific app framework & third-party apps like iHeartRadio, TuneIn, Slacker, NPR, Pandora
  • Resolution of multimedia playback & internet radio streaming issues
  • Defining & validating specifications, implementing functionality & enhancement & comprehensive testing including defect triage

Impact

  • Enhanced functionality for in-car radio system through integration of popular third party applications and seamless streaming of internet content.
