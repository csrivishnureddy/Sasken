Rugged device OEMs customize the retail experience

Written by Admin | Sep 26, 2016 4:00:00 AM

Scope

  • Replace Windows on ruggedized handset with Android and ensure
    commercialization

Solution

  • Analysis of OEM hardware design
  • OMAP4 Linux kernel and Android Jelly Bean bring-up
    • HAL porting for sensors, camera, modem integration etc.
  • Additional enhancements to Android framework
    • Multiple key layouts, key wedging, scanner support, battery hot swap
      feature, accessory peripherals and docking stations
  • System testing, validation and certifications

Impact

Enabled customer to launch Windows 8 and Android based phone simultaneously
