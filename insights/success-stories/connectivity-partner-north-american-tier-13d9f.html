Connectivity partner for a North American Tier 1

Written by Admin | Dec 24, 2019 5:00:00 AM

Scope:

  • Customer was looking for a partner with extensive connectivity and system software expertise to augment its core development team.
  • Technologies: TI J5/J5Eco/J6; QNX 6.5 OS, Android

Solution:

  • The work involved productization of 8 different variants of head units on two operating systems. Sasken worked on:
    • Feature enablement: CarPlay
    • System Drivers: Upgrading and integrating USB drivers
    • Middleware: Integrating of mode manager for handling complex mode switching, allocation of audio resources, and automatic volume control based on temperature
    • Connectivity: CAN, Ethernet AVB, Bluetooth service layer integration & testing
    • Applications: BT application development, back-end CarPlay application
    • Base port: Kernel upgrade from 3.8 to 3.14 and Android M update

Impact:

Sasken’s expertise enabled customer to launch the solution within the deadlines specified by the OEM
