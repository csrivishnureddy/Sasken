Business Success Stories - Smart TV App Development | Sasken

Written by Admin | Sep 26, 2016 4:00:00 AM

Scope

  • New Smart TV powered by Android operating system on ARM based chipset targeting European market with requirement of features/applications like Netflix, NetTV, IP-EPG, Miracast, DIAL services, Cloud Services, Skype, VOD, DLNA. ODM partnered with Sasken as technology partner to take care from conceptualization to launch and beyond
  • Middleware, Application development & UI enhancements to next-gen DTV

Solution

  • Applications: App gallery, Media playback, Remote Controller Apps, Content Browser,  Smart TV Connectivity Apps for smart phones/tablets, IP EPG, OAD(Over The Air Download)
  • Middleware: Integration of FFMPEG, DLNA, DRM, DVB-T/S to Google TV Framework. Customization &  Integration of various Multimedia modules (2D/3D ) into Google TV Framework
  • Kernels and drivers: Android kernel space and HAL boot loaders, BSP, DDs such as i2c, SPI, Timer, camera, gesture control, sensors, etc. Modules like storage, connectivity, power management & game controller
  • Testing : System and Sanity testing, Both manual and automated system testing activities
  • Certification and compliance : DLNA, DRM (WM-DRM,WB-DRM, PlayReady, Marlin, Widevine), CTS, MHP- Adaptive, DTS, HbbTV
  • Conceptualization : Web Based TV Server & Web tools development and integration activities

Impact

Helped customer entrench itself as a leading ODM in the consumer electronics sphere
