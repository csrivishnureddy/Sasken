Project management dashboard for retail stores

Written by Admin | Sep 26, 2016 4:00:00 AM

Scope

Warehouses and retail stores typically have problems related to stock management

Solution

The tablet application provides analytics and market insights for the following:

  • Aggregates data from various sources, for example, project server, defect tracking systems, financial systems
  • Visual reports to track schedule, quality, effort and financial health of projects
  • Summary and drill down views that provide holistic as well as granular data

Impact

  • Reduction in manual effort spent for reporting
  • Improved governance, thereby improving success rate of projects
